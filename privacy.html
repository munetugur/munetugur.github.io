Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

snackplay is a collection of free browser games. This page explains what data is involved when you play.

Local storage

Games save your best score locally in your browser (via localStorage). This data stays on your device — it is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

Advertising

This site is, or will be, supported by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more and manage your ad preferences at Google's ad policy page and opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic and which games people play, using cookies to distinguish visitors. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

No accounts

You never need to create an account or provide personal information to play any game on this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.
