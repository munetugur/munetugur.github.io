snackplay

Quick, free browser games you can play in a spare minute. No install, no account.

Pick by how much time you've got:

~10–15 sec Quick Draw Test your reaction time over 3 rounds. Wait for green, tap fast, don't jump the gun. ~20 sec Mole Rush Whack moles as they pop up. Gold ones are worth 5x — 20 seconds on the clock. ~20–40 sec Pancake Tower Tap to stack pancakes as they swing by. One misstep and the tower shrinks — perfect for a kettle-boiling break. ~15–45 sec Flap Tap to fly between the gaps. Squeeze in close to the edges for a near-miss bonus. ~30 sec Word Dash Type each word as fast as you can before the clock runs out. Words get harder as you build a streak. ~30–60 sec Beat Match Tap the note right as it hits the line. Three misses and the beat stops. ~30 sec – 2 min Asteroid Dash Dodge and blast your way through falling asteroids. One hit ends the run — how long can you survive? ~1–2 min Fruit Drop Drop matching fruit into columns to merge them. Chain merges for bonus points. ~1–3 min Pattern Panic Watch the pattern, then repeat it back. It gets faster every round. ~2–4 min Number Sprint Classic tile-merging, but you only get 30 moves. How high a score can you squeeze out?
