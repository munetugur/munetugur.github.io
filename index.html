snackplay

Quick, free browser games you can play in a spare minute. No install, no account.

Pick by how much time you've got:

~20–40 sec Pancake Tower Tap to stack pancakes as they swing by. One misstep and the tower shrinks — perfect for a kettle-boiling break. ~30 sec – 2 min Asteroid Dash Dodge and blast your way through falling asteroids. One hit ends the run — how long can you survive? ~2 – 4 min Number Sprint Classic tile-merging, but you only get 30 moves. How high a score can you squeeze out?
