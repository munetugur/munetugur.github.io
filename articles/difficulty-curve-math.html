The math behind Asteroid Dash's difficulty curve

Asteroid Dash has no levels and no boss fights — the entire game is one continuous run that gets harder the longer you survive. Here's exactly how that ramp works.

The two numbers that matter

Every 10 seconds of survival, two things shift:

fallSpeed = 100 * 1.05^(elapsed / 10)
spawnInterval = max(0.5, 1.2 * 0.97^(elapsed / 10))

Asteroids start falling at 100 pixels/second and speed up 5% every 10 seconds. They start spawning every 1.2 seconds and spawn slightly more often over time, down to a floor of one every half-second — past that point, more frequent spawns stopped feeling fair in testing.

Why exponential, not linear

A linear ramp feels flat for the first half of a run and then suddenly brutal. An exponential curve with a small per-step multiplier (1.05x, 0.97x) feels almost unchanged moment-to-moment but compounds into a real difference by the one-minute mark — which is exactly where we want a typical run to end.

Scoring

Score is 10 points per second survived plus 5 points per asteroid destroyed. Survival is worth more than aggression on purpose — dodging is always a valid strategy, shooting is a bonus, not a requirement.
