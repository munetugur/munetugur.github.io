Getting onto itch.io and GameJolt without giving up our own ads

A site with ten games and no traffic is just ten games nobody's played yet. Beyond word of mouth and search, we looked at listing on established game directories — places people already go looking for something quick to play.

Why itch.io and GameJolt

Both are open submission — no review queue, no gatekeeping, free to list. More importantly for us: you upload your own build, and whatever's inside it is what runs, including our own ad code. The directory just becomes another door into the same site, without handing our monetization over to someone else's platform.

Why not the bigger platforms

Some of the larger game portals require integrating their SDK and, in exchange for the extra traffic they can offer, ask you to run their ads instead of your own. That's not unreasonable on their part, but it would mean this project stops being about the site we're building and starts being about a revenue share on somebody else's platform. Worth revisiting later if this is working and we want more scale — not the first move.

What actually changes for players

Nothing. Whether you find a game through itch.io, GameJolt, or directly on this site, it's the same build, same rules, same scores saved locally on your device. The directories are just additional front doors.

The full lineup lives here regardless: snackplay.
